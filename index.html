Overview

Tabs let people switch between related content without leaving the page, keeping the interface compact and easy to scan.

Features

  • Full keyboard support (roving tabindex)
  • ARIA roles, states and relationships
  • Respects prefers-reduced-motion
  • Deep linking through the URL hash

Pricing

This component is free to use and modify. No dependencies, no build step.

FAQ

Does it work without JavaScript? Yes. All panels are shown stacked and the links jump to them.
